## Conference

- ACM Conference on Information and Knowledge Management (CIKM) 2026
- Rome, Italy, November 7–11, 2026
- Scope: artificial intelligence, data science, databases, information retrieval, knowledge management
- In-person only; at least one author must present on-site. No pre-recorded videos.

## Short Paper Definition

> Short papers should describe ongoing work, recent insights, or concise and well-founded research contributions, including preliminary but empirically validated ideas, that address research problems targeting top-tier research venues. Short papers should be particularly well suited to poster presentations.

Implication: single clear contribution + empirical validation; preliminary results acceptable if validated; fits poster format.

## Topics of Interest

### Data Lifecycle
- Data acquisition and preprocessing: data crawling, IoT data, data quality, data privacy, mitigating biases, data wrangling
- Integration and aggregation: semantic processing, data provenance, data linkage, data fusion, knowledge graphs, data warehousing, privacy and security, modeling, information credibility
- Efficient data processing: serverless, data-intensive computing, database systems, indexing and compression, architectures, distributed data systems, dataspaces, customized hardware
- Special data processing: multilingual text, sequential, stream, spatio-temporal, (knowledge) graphs, multimedia, scientific, social media data

### Analytics / ML / Neural
- Analytics and machine learning: OLAP, data mining, ML/AI, scalable analysis algorithms, algorithmic biases, event detection and tracking, understanding, interpretability
- Neural information and knowledge processing: graph neural networks, domain adaptation, transfer learning, network architectures, neural ranking, neural recommendation, neural prediction

### Information Access & IR
- Information access and retrieval: ad hoc and web search, facets and entities, question answering and dialogue systems, retrieval models, query processing, personalization, recommender and filtering systems
- Information retrieval in the era of LLMs
- Open-ended QA systems

### Users / Interfaces / Presentation
- User behavior analysis, user interface design, perception of biases, personalization, interactive information retrieval, interactive analysis, spoken interfaces
- Data presentation: visualization, summarization, readability, VR, speech input/output

### Evaluation & Crowdsourcing
- Evaluation, performance studies, benchmarks: online and offline evaluation, best practices
- Crowdsourcing: task assignment, worker reliability, optimization, trustworthiness, transparency, best practices

### Multi-modal Content Understanding
- NLP, speech recognition, computer vision, content understanding, knowledge extraction, knowledge graphs, knowledge representations

### Knowledge Graphs
- Knowledge graphs support data representation and manipulation
- Generation of knowledge graphs using unstructured data

### Responsible AI
- Fairness, Accountability, Transparency, Ethics, Explainability in Information and Knowledge Management

### Generative AI
- GenAI for Data and Knowledge Management
- GenAI for structured and unstructured data processing
- GenAI for data synthesis and simulation
- GenAI for information summarization, content creation, visualization

### Applications
- Urban systems, biomedical and health informatics, legal informatics, crisis informatics, computational social science, data-enabled discovery, social media

## Page Limit

> Short papers cannot exceed 4 pages, including an appendix, plus unlimited pages for the GenAI Usage Disclosure section and references.

- Main content + appendix: ≤ 4 pages (hard cap, combined)
- GenAI Usage Disclosure: unlimited, not counted
- References: unlimited, not counted

## Template & Format

- ACM sigconf template, 2-column
- Template: https://www.acm.org/publications/proceedings-template
- LaTeX: `\documentclass[sigconf,natbib=true,anonymous=true]{acmart}`
- Word: Interim Template
- Submit PDF via EasyChair → track "CIKM 2026 Short Paper"
- Required: ACM CCS concepts, keywords

## Double-Blind Anonymization

Submissions not properly anonymized are desk-rejected without review.

Required:
- No author names or affiliations
- Anonymized acknowledgments
- Third-person self-citations ("In [X], the authors propose...")
- Anonymized code/data links (e.g., anonymous GitHub)
- No author info in PDF metadata or filename

## Pre-Print / arXiv Policy

Non-anonymous preprints on arXiv, institutional servers, or personal sites are permitted at any time (not required, not encouraged).

Conditions (failure → desk-reject):
- Must disclose existence (or planned existence) of non-anonymous version in the EasyChair submission form
- Preprint title and abstract must be sufficiently different from the CIKM submission
- Recommended: finalize title for archival version, submit to CIKM under alternative title, modify after acceptance; same for abstract
- Track chairs have final authority in disputes

Reviewers are instructed not to search for preprints and to disclose accidental exposure.

## GenAI Usage Disclosure (Mandatory Section)

> All submissions must comply with the ACM Policy on the use of AI. Moreover, they are all required to include a "GenAI Usage Disclosure" section at the end of the limited pages of main contents and appendix, right before the references.

- Position: after main content + appendix, before references
- Not counted toward page limit
- Must disclose all GenAI use across research (code, data) and writing
- Specify: tools used, stage (ideation/writing/code/data/figure), extent (brainstorming/draft/polishing/full), author verification approach
- ACM Policy: https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/frequently-asked-questions#h-can-i-use-generative-ai-software-tools-to-prepare-my-manuscript

## Other Policies

- Double submission prohibited: no prior publication; no concurrent submission to other venues or other CIKM 2026 tracks
- Authorship fixed at abstract deadline; no changes after, including at camera-ready
- Automated plagiarism/falsification checks may be used
- Alt text for figures/tables strongly encouraged
- At least one author must register by Author Registration Deadline; otherwise paper may be removed from proceedings
